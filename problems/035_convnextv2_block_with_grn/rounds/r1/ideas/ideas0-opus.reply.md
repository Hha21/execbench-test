### Notes
- **The fixed cost per call at small sizes is the lever.** I ran `predict_score` with two simple models: every size costing 10 µs + bytes/1.5 TB/s scores **0.88**, and 25 µs + bytes/0.8 TB/s still scores 0.75. Both beat the leader's 0.69.
- **The leader seems to have a fixed floor of about 30 µs.** Its times barely change with FLOPs at small sizes (30.6 µs for 0.46 GFLOP, 35.2 µs for 0.92 GFLOP), and its throughput stays at or below 174 TF/s.
- **New budget fact for the two largest workloads.** The fp32 SIMT pipes handle 28.4 T instructions/s at 1.5 GHz. That costs about **10.8 µs per instruction per hidden element** at 64,96,112,112 and 2.4 µs at 32,128,64,64. The exact-erf GELU (about 15 instructions, one of them on the special-function unit) therefore costs about 160 µs per pass at the largest size. There, GELU once and the depthwise conv off the SIMT pipes matter more than DRAM traffic. A no-MUFU minimax Φ polynomial with error ≤1e-6 (about 9 FMA) is a separate small lever.
- **Suggested seeds:** idea 3 (or 1), idea 10, plus one long shot (6 or 5).
