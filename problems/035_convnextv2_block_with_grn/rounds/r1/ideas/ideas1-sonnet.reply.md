### Notes
- The LEADER GAP "leader µs" column is Tb-scaled by construction, because it assumes the same S on every workload. The real leader may be far faster on some sizes and slower on others, so don't treat 30–90 µs as the target.
- `predict_score`: S −85%, M −90%, L −50% gives 0.644. All three bands −90% gives 0.810. A 10% cut in one band is worth about +0.007.
- 14 of the 16 workloads move ≤29 MB, so the score is mostly set by launch count, weight ingest and the GRN dependency, not by L-size bandwidth. FP16 and TF32 both pass tolerance, so the tensor rate is not the limit anywhere.
- The two biggest sizes need the dwconv at the FMA bound and the hidden tensor kept on-chip.
