### Notes
At the leader's level the score is set by the fixed floor, not by GEMM speed. `predict_score` gives 0.86 for a profile of 15 µs plus bytes at 2 TB/s on every row, and 0.65 for 35 µs plus bytes at 0.4 TB/s, which is about where lineages A and B are today. Thirteen rows have Tb of 68–222 µs, so pulling them from about 35 µs to about 12 µs is worth about +0.12 of mean score. The top two rows are worth about +0.03 if they land at 100–400 µs.

Every phase in A and B costs 7–17 µs because it is a chain of dependent global round trips. The ideas below therefore try to:
- drop whole phases and barriers;
- keep the hidden tensor on-chip;
- read every weight byte from DRAM exactly once;
- move the SIMT-bound parts (conv and erf GELU) off the critical path at the two largest sizes.

One finding should be shared across lineages: the GRN scale tolerates a relative error in nf of about 6e-3, because gw is only about 0.04. So the GRN statistics can be computed much more cheaply than the output.
