### Notes
Ideas 1-3 and 6 are changes to lineages A and B. Ideas 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10 are new designs.

- **Lineage A** is already near the implied leader times on 1,96 / 8,192 / 32,768 / 32,128 / 64,96. It is 1.3-1.7x slower on the C=768 and 14x14 rows (59/57/70 us against 35/44/58, and 70 against 47). Its cost there is launch chains: K1 staging, K2, K3 and K4 take 20-45 us per row.
- **Score value:** each 10 us removed from a middle row is worth about +0.003. Removing a launch chain from 12 rows is worth +0.03 to +0.06. The two largest rows together are worth about +0.02.
- **Constraints:** no PDL, one cooperative or plain launch per phase set, weights converted inside the call.
- **Precision:** every precision claim should be checked with the fp64 simulation the lineages already use.
