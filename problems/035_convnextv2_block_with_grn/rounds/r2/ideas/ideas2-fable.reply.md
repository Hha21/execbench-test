Score leverage sits in the 13 rows with Tb 68–222 µs: cutting a 30 µs row to 20 µs is worth about +0.008 mean each, while halving the two largest rows is worth under +0.01 together. Three structural facts drive the ideas: hidden-channel slicing makes the GRN norm local (only a per-image scalar crosses CTAs); the erf GELU costs about 130 µs of SIMT time at the largest row, so recompute designs pay it twice; and the leader's L-row times coincide with lineage A's rented L rows while its S rows are 25% below A's. Ideas are meant to be combined by per-shape dispatch (1 or 3 for C ≤ 192, 7 or 8 for 2/8,768, 2 or 10 for the rest).
