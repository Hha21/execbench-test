### Audit

The biggest problem is where the effort is pointed. Most of the 0.07 gap to the leader is on the rows with small images (14×14 and 7×7, C=384–768). I compared each row's best rented time ×1.28 with the leader's time, using `S ≈ (Tb−Ts)/(t+Tb−2Ts)`. On that basis our projected scores are 0.48–0.62 on six rows: 8,384; 16,384; 2,768; 8,768; 16,768; 32,768. The leader scores 0.69 on each. That is about 0.045 of mean score. We already beat the leader at 1,96, 32,128 and 64,96. Three premises steer effort away from those rows:

- **Portal = rented × 1.28 (H21).** This was measured only on the 8 rows that #65570 timed, and none of them is a small-image row. It also comes from one rented session. The v3fix session, which differs only in host code, ran about 6 µs slower on the S rows. That would put the ratio near 1.12.
- **Staging and post-sync rounds cost 2–4 µs at about 8 GB/s per SM.** I read lineage A's code (`k1_dw`). It stages the 7×7/14×14 planes with one 4-byte `cp.async` per element plus a div/mod each. Our own probes contradict a hardware explanation: dependent loads cost 0.44 µs per step, and a one-shot bulk copy reaches about 120 GB/s per SM. At those rates a 12–32 KB round should take about 0.5 µs, not 2–4 µs. Lineage B's "about 20 µs floor" and C's "serial rounds" verdicts rest on this unexplained rate.
- **cuBLASLt is not the limit (H15).** That was shown at 1,192, where the custom launches dominate. At C=768 the two GEMMs take 15.6–24 µs rented. The weights are 19 MB fp32 (2.7 µs to stream) and the maths is 0.5–8 µs.

Precision is a compliance risk that the card understates. "About 5σ" describes the rms error. The quantity that matters is the largest error over up to 77M elements per round. That reached 2.37–2.39e-3 against atol 2.4e-3, and it was checked over only 1–3 rounds. Lineage C (r2-e3) did not log it at all. Under the operator's "meets the tolerance with margin" policy, a single element over atol means that row passes only through the 98% matched ratio.

H23 (switching to tcgen05) is overvalued. Applying the formula row by row gives about +0.006 of mean, not +0.02–0.03. Lineage A already gets tcgen05 through cuBLASLt. At the top two rows, cuBLASLt's GEMM time (129 µs for 802816×384×96) is set by reading and writing the hidden tensor, not by the tensor-core rate.
