### Notes
If we take the best lineage on each row and scale rented times by 1.28 to get portal times, the projected score is about **0.624**. The leader is at 0.690, so we are short about 1.07 of summed per-row score. About **76% of that gap is in six wide-C, small-image rows**: 8,384,14,14; 16,384,14,14; 2/8/16,768,7,7; and 32,768,7,7. On those rows we are 1.45–2.1× the leader's implied times, and our per-row scores are 0.51–0.60. On the two largest rows we already beat the leader. Those rows still pay well, because their Tb is large.

Two facts from the evidence drive most of the ideas:
- After a grid sync, each per-thread L2 load round takes 2–4 µs (about 4–8 B/clk/SM). One-shot bulk copies run at about 120 GB/s/SM. The S rows scale exactly with the clock, so this cost is in cycles: instruction issue and memory-level parallelism.
- The exact erf GELU costs about 15 SIMT instructions plus one MUFU per hidden element. At 64,96 that is about 160 µs of SIMT per GELU pass at 1.5 GHz. So recomputing GELU is not free there.

All rented→portal conversions below use ×1.28.
