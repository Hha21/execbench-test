- 1,192: dS/dt is about -0.0055 per µs, so 10 µs is worth about 0.055 of that row.
- 2,768: about -0.0031 per µs.
- 8,320: about -0.002 per µs.
- 32,128: about -0.0006 per µs.
- 64,96: about -0.00014 per µs.
