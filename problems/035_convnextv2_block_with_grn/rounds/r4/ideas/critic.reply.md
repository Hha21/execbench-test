### Audit

I read the r2-lib-cublaslt-fp16-tmaconv card, which includes the per-row portal table from #65878. The r3-merge record was too large to open without a shell, so I worked out its per-row dispatch by matching its portal times against each lineage's rented times. Treat those path attributions as inferred.

1. **The uniform ×1.28 portal/rented ratio is wrong now that we have 16 rows (H21, H11, the r3 notes).** For lineage A, portal ÷ rented is 1.16–1.22 on the small rows (1,96 1.16; 1,192 1.20; 2,96 and 2,768 1.22) and 1.23–1.27 on most others. The single-kernel paths in the merge come out at 1.25–1.26 for B and about 1.32 for D. So when a fused kernel beats A on the rented card, much of that lead disappears on the portal: at 1,192 B is 15% faster rented but only 10.5% faster on the portal, and the notebook shows S band −8.5% bench against −4.0% portal. This matches tricks.md's #35 r8 lesson, and it means S-row projections for fused designs are too optimistic.

2. **The top two rows are being ranked by their gap to the leader, not by how much faster they could go.** H3 is parked "because we lead", and H23 is valued at +0.005–0.010 by counting only GEMM time. But at 64,96 the separate K2 pass alone takes 447 µs rented, moving 1.23 GB at 2.76 TB/s, and at 32,128 it takes 113 µs. Both rows run 16–19× above their stream floor.
   - Removing K2 is worth +0.086 and +0.091 per row, about **+0.011 of mean**.
   - Just running K2 at 6 TB/s is worth about +0.006.
   
   The score is an absolute per-row formula, so being ahead of the leader on a row does not make improving it worthless. Halving 64,96 is worth 70% as much as halving 8,768.

3. **"The fusion idea is not dead, the engine is" overstates things for the S rows (E1's next step).** At 1,192, the fused GEMM1f takes 12–14 µs, about the same as cuBLASLt GEMM1 plus K2 (5.1 + 9.8 µs). The MMA work is roughly 3–5 µs of that, so a tcgen05 rewrite would mainly help the M-heavy rows.

4. **The "~20 µs cooperative floor" that retires lineage B's thesis is overcounted.** The 2.6–3.5 µs cold rounds were applied to rounds that come after a grid sync, but those read data just written to L2, which costs about 0.8 µs per 32 KB. Rebuilding the floor gives 4.6 + 2 × 1.8 + one cold round + 2 × 0.9 ≈ 11–14 µs. The 1.5–2.0 µs barrier release was also measured with a single barrier implementation, against 0.1–0.2 µs per atomic in the literature.

5. **The precision margin is still unmeasured on the kernel we shipped.** The max error is 2.39e-3 against an atol of 2.4e-3, measured over 1–3 rounds only. The 10-seeded-round log has been skipped four times, including for the merge's D rows, which round differently.

6. **The merge's 8,768 path looks like a pick based on noise.** On the portal it took 83.8 µs, against 80.1 µs for r2-lib, while every other shared row matched within 1%.
