### Notes
Most of the remaining score sits in two places. The first is the six wide-C small-image rows, worth up to about +0.066 if a tcgen05 kernel reaches 28–54 µs on the portal. The second is the largest rows, where we already beat the leader but are far from the floor: S is not capped at the leader. In lineage A at 64,96, K2 alone (GELU and statistics) takes 447 µs rented against a 175 µs DRAM floor, and the conv takes 315 µs against about 105 µs at the FMA bound. Three changes, ideas 6, 8 and 4, are a low-risk package worth about +0.035 together.

On precision: the GRN statistics barely affect the output. They are averaged over the image's tokens, divided by their own mean, and multiplied by gw ≈ 0.04, so an error in gf reaches y at about 0.04×. The output path must still use the exact erf GELU.

I believe cuBLASLt's GELU epilogue uses the tanh form, so it should not be used. CUTLASS `GELU<T>` uses erf (`GELU_taylor` is the tanh one). Check both before relying on them.
