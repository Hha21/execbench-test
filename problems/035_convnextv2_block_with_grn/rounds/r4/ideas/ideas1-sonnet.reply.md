### Notes
Value sits in two places. The six wide-C rows hold 0.056 of mean, and one 8,768-type row is worth +0.0098 if it reaches the leader. The S rows hold about 0.02. The top two rows are worth about +0.02 together. Two numbers drive the ideas:
- At S/M rows, K2 and K3 cost 8–19 and 6–17 µs because each launch is a chain of 2–3 dependent rounds. The count of custom launches matters more than their bytes.
- At 64,96 there are 308M GELU elements. An SIMT erf at about 15 FMA-pipe ops each is about 200 µs on the FMA pipe, on top of a conv of about 105 µs. That is why K2 takes 447 µs.

Ideas 1, 2, 3 and 10 are the engine and fusion lines. 4–6 are cheap fixed-cost levers. 7–9 are long shots.
