BRIEF FROM THE OPERATOR FOR THIS ROUND:
Operator ruling today: plain TF32 tensor-core compute (fp32 accumulate, fp32 in/out) is now allowed on #35. See the constraint and the sol.yaml note. r0's portal anchors refute two ledger beliefs. First, Tb is not near the fp32 reference: it runs at TF32 speed (82.8 us at B=5, 90.7 at 128, 123 at 512, 169.8 at 919 = 613 TFLOP/s). Second, Tsol is not at the FP32 CUDA-core peak: it is about 15 us at B<=211 and rises to 57.9 us at 919, which is about 1.8 PFLOP/s, a bf16-like rate. Exact-fp32 paths therefore cannot score at M/L. Retire the 3xTF32 / BF16x9 lines, and plan the round around plain TF32. Two ends of the range matter. At small B, stream the 226 MB weight at about 7 TB/s (Tb is about 83 us there, so the copy floor of about 32 us at the lock scores about 0.75). At large B, TF32 tcgen05 GEMMs near peak (cuBLASLt FAST_TF32 measured 50-135 us rented; a fused CUTLASS/CuTe tcgen05 TF32 kernel with the bias and six-way split in the epilogue). Watch the tolerance margin (TF32 max error 1.6e-3 vs atol 2e-3): K is fixed at 3072, so keep fp32 accumulation over the full K and do not split K into low-precision partials.
